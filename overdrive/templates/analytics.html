How to read these numbers

These are creation-cohort metrics, not the number of sales closed during the selected dates. Archives are included by default so archiving a lost deal does not erase it from the results. Current unit membership controls access, including to history; a transferred deal is attributed to its current unit.

Advance rates count each deal once per stage, even after repeated visits. They are not a forecast: open deals may still advance later. The duration chart counts each completed stay separately, ending on a stage change, closure or archive. Reopening or restoring starts a new stay. Time to win measures elapsed time from observed creation to the latest observed win, including any pauses. Missing observations are shown as unknown, never zero.
